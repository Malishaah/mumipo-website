Meet Mumipo

Mumipo is a fluffy little explorer. A garden path or a single strawberry can turn into a whole adventure.

Mumipo and the Mysterious Shadow

Mumipo’s first adventure

Who’s following Mumipo down the garden path?

Something on the Path

One sunny morning, Mumipo waddled down the garden path. Then he stopped. A little dark shape sat beside his feet. It had a little beak just like his!

Mumipo stepped left. The shape came too. He stepped right. There it was again! His tiny wings fluttered. He tried to run, but his tiny feet got tangled.

Flump! He landed in a soft, fluffy heap.

Hop, Wiggle, Hop!

Mumipo stood up and lifted one little foot. The shape lifted a foot too. He put his foot down. So did the shape.

It was his own shadow!

Mumipo hopped. His shadow hopped. He wiggled, and his shadow wiggled too. Then a cloud drifted in front of the sun, and the little shadow faded away. Mumipo looked left. He looked right. Where had it gone?

The Shadow That Stayed

Out came the sun. There was his little shadow again! But beside it lay another shadow. A much, much bigger one.

Mumipo took one tiny step. His little shadow moved. The big one stayed right where it was.

Slowly, Mumipo looked up.
